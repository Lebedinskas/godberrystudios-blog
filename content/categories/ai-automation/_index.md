Automation used to mean wiring triggers to actions. In 2026 it means deciding how much judgment to hand an AI agent, and which platform to trust with it. The posts here are hands-on tests of those platforms and stacks — built on the same afternoon, with no platform loyalty to defend.

The honest summary: most "AI agent" features shipped in the last year are real improvements, but the right tool still depends on whether you can self-host, how you are billed per operation, and how much you need a non-developer to maintain it. The wrong choice is expensive to unwind once workflows pile up.

For the platform decision, start with [n8n vs Make vs Zapier for AI agents](/posts/n8n-vs-make-vs-zapier-ai-agents-2026/). For a practical task-by-task view, see [automating business tasks with AI](/posts/automate-business-tasks-with-ai-2026/) and [free AI tools that replace expensive software](/posts/free-ai-tools-replace-expensive-software-2026/).
