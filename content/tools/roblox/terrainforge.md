Generate full landscapes in one click. Six biomes — Islands, Mountains, Plains, Desert, Tundra, Volcanic — built from multi-octave Perlin noise, thermal erosion, per-biome material layers, water, and procedural vegetation.

Everything is **seed-reproducible**, so you can dial in a world and recreate it exactly. The first piece of the world-builder suite: **TerrainForge** shapes the land, **RoadForge** lays the roads, **ScatterForge** dresses it with props.
